# What's new in Mochiboard

Each update gets a short line here. Mochiboard shows the new ones in Settings when an update is ready.
Write them for people, not for code: one line, what changed and why it's nice.

## October 2026

- Design thinking templates in the toolbar: affinity map, opportunity map, impact / effort, empathy map, journey map, how might we, and rose, thorn, bud. Each comes with an example and a how-to note from Mochi.
- Mochiboard works without internet. Everything on the board keeps working; Mochi just tells you she'll think again once you're back online.
- Canvases can go anywhere: drag one onto another board, onto the open board, into a folder, or right-click and make it its own board.
- Settings has tabs now (General, Mochi, Helpers, Board, About), so it's easier to find things.
- See how much Claude Mochi has used, in Settings → Helpers, with a link to your plan's limits.
- Peach boards get their color dot back in the sidebar.
- You can download Mochiboard straight from jessa.art now, no Terminal needed. It runs on Apple chip and Intel Macs.
- Group anything together so it moves as one: select a few things, then Group (or ⌘G). Right-click to ungroup.
- Updates now live in Settings: Mochiboard tells you when there's a new version and updates with one tap.
- Folders can live inside folders. Drag one onto another in the sidebar or on Home.
- Copy and paste cards between boards with ⌘C and ⌘V. Collections keep their cards.
- Unsorted always fits on screen now, and its list scrolls.
- A friendlier Settings page, with a note from the person who made Mochiboard.
