# What's new in Mochiboard

Each update gets a short line here. Mochiboard shows the new ones in Settings when an update is ready.
Write them for people, not for code: one line, what changed and why it's nice.

## October 2026 · 1.4.1

- Phone-shaped video recordings stay centered on every camera.

## October 2026 · 1.4.0

- Pick a font for notes, titles, checklists and collections: any font on your Mac, with a live preview as you hover.
- Font menus start with the fonts already used on your board, including inside docs and wireframes, so it's easy to stay consistent.
- Voice memos wait for you: drop one on the board and press Start recording when you're ready. Nothing records by surprise.
- The grid can be dots, crosses, little x's, hearts, triangles, squares or sparkles. Pick one in Settings → Board.
- Video cards: record yourself with your camera (press V), wide or phone-shaped for reels and stories, or drop in a video file. Videos play right on the board, and you can scrub and mute them.
- Opening Settings lets go of whatever you had selected or were holding.
- Make the toolbar yours: right-click it (or go to Settings → General) to hide tools you don't use and drag the rest into your own order.

## October 2026 · 1.3.2

- A quiet bar at the top of your board tells you when a new version is ready. Update now, or hide it until the next one. No more pop-ups.
- Updates can happen by themselves: say yes once and new versions slip in when you quit Mochiboard. Change it anytime in Settings → General.

## October 2026 · 1.3.1

- Just the board: press ⌘\ (or the corners button by the zoom) to hide every panel. ⌘\ or Esc brings them back.
- GIFs keep moving when you add them to a board, and see-through PNGs stay see-through.
- Boards added from outside the app (like from Claude Code) show up right away, and Mochi tells you where to find them.
- Right-click while you're holding something (a sticker, a note to place, a shape) to put it down, just like Esc.

## October 2026 · 1.3.0

- Switches in Settings show the Mochiboard pointer instead of a text cursor.
- Colors and the dot grid live under Settings → Board now.
- A phone layout: the board fills the screen, panels slide up from a hub at the bottom, and fingers pan, pinch and press-and-hold.
- The action bar's icons match the toolbar's, in size and color.
- Mochi finds Claude Code wherever it's installed, notices right away when you set it up, and explains when it isn't signed in. "Set up" opens a step-by-step guide.
- The window can shrink much smaller, and its edges show the Mac's resize arrows.
- The left panels' shadows fade softly instead of stopping in a hard edge.
- Docs are real documents now: styles (Title, Heading, Lead, Caption, Quote…), any font on your Mac, sizes, bold/italic/underline, colors and highlights from your board, alignment, lists, checklists, links, pictures, tables and line spacing.
- Select words in a doc and ask Mochi to make them shorter, clearer, friendlier, or anything you type.
- Cards dropped into a doc keep their shape: a checklist stays a checklist, a link stays a link.
- Download any doc as a PDF or a Word file (.docx), on Letter or A4 pages. Word files keep headings, lists, tables, links and pictures, with DM Sans built in.
- No more Mac beep when you delete things or use shortcuts on the board.
- In dark mode, the "pull colors" button on pictures is easy to read.

## October 2026 · 1.2.1

- Click a layer and Mochi points it out on the board: a pink glow and a little wave from its corner.

## October 2026 · 1.2.0

- Your own cursor: your name and a color you pick follow your mouse around the board.
- Your colors: change the board, dots, panels and accent in Settings, or right-click any board for its own background.
- Board colors: right-click a saved color to rename, change or remove it.
- Layers: everything on the board in one list. Pick, rename, hide, lock and drag to restack.
- Resize wireframe pieces: drag a piece's corner to reshape it, or double-click to reach inside a button or input.
- The resize corner always sits on top and stays the same size at any zoom.
- Drawing sizes: a slider for any thickness (and [ ] while drawing). The eraser has a size too.
- All 48 stamps are pixel art now.
- The starter boards (Moodboard, App plan, Brain dump) moved into the Templates menu.
- Smoother dragging and zooming on big boards, and the dots stay the same size as you zoom.

## Earlier in October 2026

- The What’s new card uses Mochiboard’s own icons.
- Dark mode fixes: the templates menu and the stamp wheel’s + button are easy to see now.
- Design thinking templates in the toolbar: affinity map, opportunity map, impact / effort, empathy map, journey map, how might we, and rose, thorn, bud. Each comes with an example and a how-to note from Mochi.
- Mochiboard works without internet. Everything on the board keeps working; Mochi just tells you she'll think again once you're back online.
- Canvases can go anywhere: drag one onto another board, onto the open board, into a folder, or right-click and make it its own board.
- Settings has tabs now (General, Mochi, Helpers, Board, About), so it's easier to find things.
- See how much Claude Mochi has used, in Settings → Helpers, with a link to your plan's limits.
- Peach boards get their color dot back in the sidebar.
- A little “What’s new” card the first time you open a new version.
- You can download Mochiboard straight from jessa.art now, no Terminal needed. It runs on Apple chip and Intel Macs.
- Group anything together so it moves as one: select a few things, then Group (or ⌘G). Right-click to ungroup.
- Updates now live in Settings: Mochiboard tells you when there's a new version and updates with one tap.
- Folders can live inside folders. Drag one onto another in the sidebar or on Home.
- Copy and paste cards between boards with ⌘C and ⌘V. Collections keep their cards.
- Unsorted always fits on screen now, and its list scrolls.
- A friendlier Settings page, with a note from the person who made Mochiboard.
