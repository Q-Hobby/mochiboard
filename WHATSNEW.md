# What's new in Mochiboard

Each update gets a short line here. Mochiboard shows the new ones in Settings when an update is ready.
Write them for people, not for code: one line, what changed and why it's nice.

## October 2026

- Updates now live in Settings: Mochiboard tells you when there's a new version and updates with one tap.
- Folders can live inside folders. Drag one onto another in the sidebar or on Home.
- Copy and paste cards between boards with ⌘C and ⌘V. Collections keep their cards.
- Unsorted always fits on screen now, and its list scrolls.
- A friendlier Settings page, with a note from the person who made Mochiboard.
